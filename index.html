About Scott and Jon

Two friends who love anything with a motor.

Scott Rissi and Jon VanderWeele met as teenagers and bonded over a love of anything with a motor.

That love led them to lawn mowers. As soon as they were both old enough to drive, they had plows on their vehicles. They have been in the landscape maintenance business since 1986. Thornapple Lawn Care got its start in 1996 and became Thornapple Inc. in 2011.

Shared interests are what make them passionate about what they do. It was never about bells and whistles. It is about doing the work well, with the right equipment, for people who count on it. We now run electric equipment too, including an electric lawn mower, and we keep looking for quieter, cleaner options.
