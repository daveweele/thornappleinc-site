About us

Owners who have done this since 1986.

Owners Scott Rissi and Jon VanderWeele have been in the landscape maintenance business since 1986. They officially combined their talents to form Thornapple Lawn Care in 1996, and incorporated as Thornapple Inc. in 2011.

Together they bring over 35 years of experience and knowledge to lawn care and snow plowing service. We now use electric equipment, including an electric lawn mower, and we keep looking for quieter, more environmentally friendly options.
